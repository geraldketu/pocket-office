# OliverFee 360 approved pricing

Basic is $49.99 per month. UCR assistance is included with no additional OliverFee service charge. Any applicable UCR registration fee is listed separately.

The Carrier Startup Bundle is a $399 one-time OliverFee 360 service fee for the approved bundle scope. Applicable government and third-party fees are listed separately and added to the final total after verification. The full total is due before processing begins.

A review request does not activate a subscription, authorize a filing, or collect payment. Stripe checkout remains disabled and requests remain unpaid.
