# Permit screening coverage - verified 2026-11-10

This is a bounded screening release, not a complete determination of all trucking laws in 50 states. Every selected operating or pass-through state has five explicit manual review categories. No empty state checklist is interpreted as permission to operate.

The live standalone application is the root Next.js app. The archived oliverfee-360/ Worker source is not this release's live onboarding.

## Verified screenings

The narrow criteria and source links below were checked against official sources on 2026-11-10. Claiming an exemption generally routes to manual review unless its complete narrow test is implemented. Required means an obligation applies; it does not mean a carrier lacks valid existing credentials or must buy a new filing.

- **IFTA / temporary fuel credentials** (Federal / multi-state): [IFTA official carrier guidance](https://ww.iftach.org/Carriers/); [Official qualified vehicle definition](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol3/dftl/dftl-60112.html); [Temporary fuel permit licensing guidance](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/product-excise-taxes/fuels-taxes-and-fees/consumer-fuels/ifta-licenses/licensing/ifta-licensing-requirements/). Temporary options: Annual IFTA is not the only credential route. Temporary fuel permits must be obtained in each jurisdiction, including the base when required; limits vary. A registration trip permit does not replace a fuel permit.

- **Unified Carrier Registration (UCR)** (Federal / multi-state): [UCR official questionnaire](https://plan.ucr.gov/do-i-need-to-register/); [UCR handbook — applicability and exemptions](https://prod-public-ucr-docs-governing-documents.s3.amazonaws.com/UCR%20Handbook%20022924.pdf). Temporary options: No general temporary-trip substitute for annual UCR was verified. Private property and exempt commodities do not automatically exempt UCR.

- **IRP apportioned registration / trip permits** (Federal / multi-state): [IRP official registration FAQ](https://www.irponline.org/resource/resmgr/education_training/faq_irp_registration_6-12-24.pdf); [Ohio BMV IRP and trip permit guidance](https://www.bmv.ohio.gov/vr-irp-geninfo.aspx). Temporary options: For occasional travel, confirm registration trip permits in each jurisdiction before entry. Fuel credentials are separate. Lighter eligible vehicles may optionally apportion.

- **Federal operating authority (MC)** (Federal / multi-state): [FMCSA operating authority and exceptions](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/get-mc-number-authority-operate). Temporary options: An application is not permission to operate. No general state trip permit substitutes for required active federal authority.

- **USDOT registration** (Federal / multi-state): [FMCSA USDOT criteria](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/do-i-need-usdot-number). Temporary options: USDOT registration is separate from operating authority, state permits and fuel credentials.

- **Kentucky KYU weight-distance tax** (KY): [Kentucky KYU weight-distance tax](https://drive.ky.gov/motor-carriers/Pages/KYU.aspx). Temporary options: A one-time temporary KYU permit per truck is available for infrequent travel. Confirm current price, eligibility and duration; permanent license issuance is free but mileage tax is separate.

- **New Mexico weight-distance tax** (NM): [New Mexico weight-distance tax](https://www.mvd.newmexico.gov/commercial/commercial-vehicles/weight-distance/). Temporary options: Confirm temporary permits and current rates with New Mexico. Rates changed July 1, 2026; no old mileage table is used.

- **Oregon weight-mile tax** (OR): [Oregon weight-mile tax](https://www.oregon.gov/odot/mct/pages/weight-mile-temporary-tax-pass.aspx). Temporary options: Temporary tax pass: $9 plus prepaid weight-mile tax; ten days or purchased mileage, with usage limits. Mileage tax and eligibility must be confirmed.

- **New York Highway Use Tax (HUT)** (NY): [NY HUT registration criteria](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/hut/certificate_of_registration.htm); [NY HUT exclusions and exemptions](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/hut/excluded_and_exempt_vehicles.htm); [NY HUT trip certificate](https://www.tax.ny.gov/pubs_and_bulls/tg_bulletins/hut/cor_trip_certificate.htm). Temporary options: A $25 HUT trip certificate is limited to ten per year and expires at midnight on the third day after issue; not available for automotive-fuel transport. It does not replace a fuel-use permit.

- **Connecticut Highway Use Fee** (CT): [Connecticut DRS highway-use classification and weight criteria](https://portal.ct.gov/DRS/Businesses/Highway-Use-Fee/HUF); [Official statutory supplement — claimed exemptions require current confirmation](https://www.cga.ct.gov/2024/sup/chap_222a.htm). Temporary options: No temporary HUF substitute was verified. Government/dairy exemption claims require current statutory confirmation.

- **California Motor Carrier Permit (MCP)** (CA): [California DMV motor carrier permits and exceptions](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/motor-carrier-services-mcs/motor-carrier-permits/); [Caltrans MCP in-state / out-of-state guidance](https://dot.ca.gov/programs/traffic-operations/legal-truck-access/motor-carrier). Temporary options: Seasonal MCP is limited to eligible intrastate for-hire carriers (6–11 months); it is not a general interstate trip permit.

- **California Clean Truck Check** (CA): [CARB Clean Truck Check official FAQ](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/clean-truck-check-faq-0); [CARB five-day pass](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/resources/fact-sheets/five-day-pass-request). Temporary options: A five-day CARB pass may be available once per vehicle per calendar year, subject to advance approval and no outstanding enforcement. Apply at least seven business days ahead. This is not a fuel or registration permit; no low-use exemption.

- **California Truck and Bus emissions** (CA): [CARB Truck and Bus basics and exemptions](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/truck-bus-regulation-basics-exemptions-truckstop). Temporary options: A claimed low-use, specialized vehicle or alternative-compliance option needs documentation review; a modern engine means compliance may be satisfied, not that the rule disappears.

- **California fuel trip permit alternative** (CA): [CDTFA California fuel trip permit](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/fuel-tax-and-fee-guides/use-fuel-tax/california-fuel-trip-permit.htm); [Official qualified vehicle definition](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol3/dftl/dftl-60112.html). Temporary options: $30 per qualifying vehicle; four consecutive days, obtain before California entry. Separate from DMV registration permits and CARB passes.

- **California hazardous materials transportation license** (CA): [CHP official guidance](https://www.chp.ca.gov/siteassets/forms/chp800c.pdf). Verified 2026-11-10. Qualifying placarded loads OR compensated qualifying material over 500 lb are screened separately; claimed exceptions need review.

## Manual review boundaries

All states retain manual checks for intrastate registration/authority; mileage, weight and other trucking taxes beyond the listed narrow rules; registration trip and fuel permit reciprocity and limits; oversize/overweight and route constraints; hazmat, specialized cargo and local compliance. The FHWA link is a verified state permit contact directory, not evidence that each state's tax, authority or hazmat rules were verified. Verify the responsible agency, source, current rule, exemption documents and date before changing these items to automatic decisions.

California MCP decisions are intentionally conservative for out-of-state carriers. CARB applicability includes pass-through; no low-use Clean Truck Check exclusion is assumed. Port/drayage, Advanced Clean Fleets, hazardous waste, local routes and special vehicle exceptions remain manual. Connecticut claimed statutory exemptions require current confirmation. New Mexico mileage rates changed in July 2026 and are not hard-coded.

IFTA and IRP use actual physical member-jurisdiction travel and vehicle-specific axle/actual-or-registered weight tests. Alaska and Hawaii do not count as members. A single-state route can still be interstate commerce for UCR/MC. A private employee passenger bus can require IFTA without UCR; a two-axle 18,000 lb interstate property truck can require UCR without IFTA. Annual registration/fuel obligations may have jurisdiction-specific temporary alternatives; those are not interchangeable with UCR, MC, emissions or tax credentials.

## Fees and processing

Basic remains $49.99/month. Basic UCR assistance adds $0 OliverFee service fee. Startup Bundle remains $399 before outside fees. Unknown fees show "Fee to be confirmed." The only new numeric government-fee calculations are a verified standard $300 single permanent MC authority application and California's $30 four-day fuel trip permit per confirmed qualifying vehicle. Both require the exact option/quantity to be answered. Other agency taxes, fuel, tests, insurance and third-party charges remain unpriced pending review. No fee total is permission to operate. Full payment is required upfront before processing; Stripe remains disabled and requests remain unpaid.

## Call guide behavior

Every checklist and selected estimate requirement uses the same keyed rule and accessible ? button. Questions to Ask the Carrier reuses shared answers, defaults to missing item-specific questions, offers review/edit of collected answers, and includes Not sure. Fleet details are editable inside the panel. Unverified rule categories stay Needs review even after all questions are answered. Answers, screening decisions, source URLs and verification dates are recomputed on the server and stored with the unpaid review snapshot.
